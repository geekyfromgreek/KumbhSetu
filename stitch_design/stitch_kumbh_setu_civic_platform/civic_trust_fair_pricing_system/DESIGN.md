## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, culturally resonant, and impeccably transparent civic platform engineered for large-scale public trust. Tailored for major public gatherings, municipal coordination, and pilgrim-facing fair-pricing verification, the visual language rejects generic tech tropes—such as neon gradients, artificial glassmorphism, or sterile software aesthetics—in favor of grounded, physical-world civic dignity.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Editorial Civic Design** with **Warm Subcontinental Materiality**. It draws inspiration from traditional public notices, printed gazettes, and stone-carved civic infrastructure while delivering razor-sharp modern legibility across high-sunlight, low-connectivity mobile environments.

### Core Brand Attributes
- **Grounded Public Authority:** Unshakable, calm, and institutional without feeling bureaucratic or intimidating.
- **Cultural Dignity:** Rooted in warm earthen tones, saffron clay, and deep ceremonial maroon that feel natural to sacred civic gatherings.
- **Radical Transparency:** Clear price caps, verified vendor credentials, emergency channels, and zero decorative deception.
- **Physical Legibility:** High contrast, deliberate tactile borders, and generous tap targets optimized for field use by diverse demographic cohorts.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in sacred civic pigments: the natural earthen ochre-saffron of temple flagstones and ceremonial riverbanks, tempered by deep heritage maroon and high-density slate for uncompromising legibility under intense daylight.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D95D1E` - Saffron Ochre):** Used for primary calls to action, active navigation states, certified fair-price badges, and essential interaction points. Never applied as ambient wash; reserved strictly for utility, verification, and critical focus.
- **Secondary (`#6B1A1E` - Heritage Maroon):** Represents municipal authority, institutional headers, regulatory stamps, and permanent civic structural elements.
- **Tertiary (`#8C6328` - Brass Accent):** Reserved for secondary badges, archival timestamps, civic advisory tags, and subtle metallic delimiters.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1F1F24` - Charcoal Slate):** The primary text color. High pigment concentration ensures absolute legibility without the harshness of pure `#000000`.
- **Neutral Subtle (`#5E5D66` - Weathered Stone):** Used for supporting editorial body text, form field labels, and metadata.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FAF8F5` - Light Cream):** The foundation background across all screen containers, providing an organic, non-dazzling reading plane reminiscent of heavy vellum or sun-cured handmade paper.
- **Surface Card (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Chalk):** Elevated surface blocks for verified rate cards, vendor permits, and interactive modules.
- **Border Neutral (`#E5DFD5` - Mortar Sand):** Architectural structural strokes that anchor cards, headers, and modular data points without generating visual noise.
- **Status Colors:**
  - **Success / Fair Rate Verified (`#1E6B38`):** Deep banyan green.
  - **Warning / Rate Discrepancy (`#B85D00`):** Mustard amber.
  - **Alert / Overcharging Violation (`#9E1A1A`):** Pure brick crimson.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the humanist geometric presence of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the meticulous structural clarity of **Inter**. 

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Headlines & Display:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans. Its open apertures, warm terminals, and sturdy vertical stems project both contemporary accessibility and civic authority without corporate sterility.
- **Body & Data Displays:** Set in Inter for maximum optical precision in tabular rate cards, official notices, multi-language price breakdowns, and dense lists.
- **Price Numerals:** All fair-pricing figures must use tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) within `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` to guarantee vertical column alignment across vendor price comparisons.
- **Bilingual & Multilingual Parity:** Line heights must remain generous (minimum 1.45x for body text) to accommodate Devanagari and regional scripts when rendered alongside or in place of English text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an architectural **8pt base grid** designed for structured civic trust. Density is disciplined: elements feel anchored, clear, and easy to parse under haste or distress.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (0 – 639px):** 4-column layout. Margin: `16px`. Gutters: `12px`. Sticky bottom navigation and rate verification sheets are anchored to the lower viewport for immediate single-thumb operation.
- **Tablet (640 – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Margin: `32px`. Gutters: `16px`. Rate verification tables reflow into two-column structured cards.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fixed-max layout centered at `1200px` (`75rem`). Margin: `48px`. Gutters: `24px`. Dedicated side rail for quick-jump regulatory zones, complaints, and official municipal gazettes.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Headings maintain a strict 3:1 margin ratio: three units of space above, one unit below to tightly tie labels to their respective data sets.
- Cards enforce an internal padding of `16px` on mobile screens, expanding to `24px` on desktop layouts.

## Elevation & Depth

To protect the platform's anti-synthetic aesthetic and guarantee legibility under direct sunlight, depth is established through **material layering and tactile borders**, rather than heavy blur-radius drop shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background `#FAF8F5`. Grounded, non-reflective base plane.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Containers):** Solid `#FFFFFF` fill bounded by a crisp 1px solid `#E5DFD5` border. No drop shadows. Depth is communicated strictly via tonal contrast against the cream canvas.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hovered Components & Floating Filters):** `#FFFFFF` fill with a dual-stage structural edge:
  - Outline: `1px solid #D9D2C7`
  - Tactile offset shadow: `0px 2px 4px rgba(31, 31, 36, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(31, 31, 36, 0.06)`
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets, Rate Calculators & Urgent Civic Banners):**
  - Outline: `1px solid #C4BCAD`
  - Shadow: `0px 8px 24px rgba(31, 31, 36, 0.10)`
- **Overlays & Backdrops:** Pure charcoal slate with 40% opacity (`rgba(31, 31, 36, 0.40)`). No backdrop filters or frosted glass blurs are permitted.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft Structural Shapes (`roundedness: 1`)**. 

All corner curves are restrained to mirror physical stampings, stone slabs, and official printed paperwork.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Base Components (`rounded-sm`):** `4px` (`0.25rem`). Applied to input fields, table rows, checkboxes, standard buttons, and small tags.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg`):** `8px` (`0.5rem`). Applied to verification modules, vendor credential cards, and dialogue containers.
- **Prominent Civic Notices (`rounded-xl`):** `12px` (`0.75rem`). Applied to major announcement panels and mobile bottom sheets.
- **Badges & Official Seals:** Circular or pill-shaped only when representing authentic certifying seals or counter pills. All functional buttons maintain the disciplined 4px radius.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Civic Button:** Solid Saffron fill (`#D95D1E`), text `#FFFFFF`, 4px radius, 48px minimum height for field accessibility. Hover state shifts to `#C24A10`. Focus ring is a 2px offset in `#6B1A1E`.
- **Secondary Institutional Button:** Solid Maroon fill (`#6B1A1E`), text `#FAF8F5`, 4px radius. Hover shifts to `#521216`.
- **Tertiary / Outlined Button:** Transparent fill, 1.5px solid border `#E5DFD5`, text `#1F1F24`. Hover transitions to `#FAF8F5` background with border `#1F1F24`.

### 2. Fair-Pricing & Rate Cards
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #E5DFD5`. Radius: `8px`.
- **Header:** Features official municipal sector categorization with category tags in deep maroon.
- **Pricing Display:** The verified fair price is displayed in 24px Plus Jakarta Sans bold with an adjacent certified badge (`#1E6B38`).
- **Footer:** Displays timestamp of latest municipal price gazette revision and a one-tap direct report action for overcharging violations.

### 3. Verification Chips & Status Badges
- **Verified Official Rate:** Background `#EBF5EE`, text `#1E6B38`, border `1px solid #B8E2C4`. Preceded by a verified checkmark icon.
- **Government Advisory:** Background `#FDF6EC`, text `#B85D00`, border `1px solid #F5DCB8`.
- **Emergency / Violations:** Background `#FDF0F0`, text `#9E1A1A`, border `1px solid #F5C6C6`.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- Minimum touch target: `48px`.
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1.5px solid #E5DFD5`. Radius: `4px`.
- Text: 16px Inter (`#1F1F24`) to prevent automatic iOS zoom behaviors.
- Active focus state: Border shifts to `#D95D1E` with an inner 1px glow or clean outline. Never neon.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20x20px dimension with a 3px radius for checkboxes; pure circular for radio elements.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid #A8A29A`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Fill `#D95D1E` with a crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark or center pip.

### 6. Lists & Data Tables
- Clean horizontal borders (`1px solid #E5DFD5`) separating list rows.
- Alternating row zebra fills are avoided; clean whitespace and 12px vertical cell padding maintain tabular legibility.
- Numerical items are right-aligned with monospace/tabular numeric properties.

### 7. Thin-Line Iconography Standards
- Icon stroke width must maintain a consistent `1.5px` or `1.75px` across all sizes.
- Never use heavy filled illustration vectors or colorful emoji indicators in civic data rows.
- Icons inherit the color token of their parent label (`#1F1F24`, `#6B1A1E`, or `#D95D1E`).
