KumbhSetu-Expo

Yatri App (Pilgrim)

For Pilgrims & Visitors

help_outline What is it used for?

This app helps pilgrims visiting Nashik and Trimbakeshwar travel safely and avoid being overcharged. Yatris use it to check government-approved auto and cab rates, find holy bath timings, find free food centers, and report any problems directly with a photo.

featured_play_list Key Features

  • check_circle Official Transit Fare Board: Calculates approved rates for 50+ routes across Nashik, CBS, Railway Station, and Trimbakeshwar.
  • check_circle Live Ghat Status & Snan Timings: Real-time crowd levels and auspicious bath hours for Ramkund and other Ghats.
  • check_circle Report Issue & SOS: Allows pilgrims to report overcharging, lost items, or medical emergencies with a photo.
  • check_circle Annakshetra Locator: Map and list of free food distribution centers, water points, and verified stalls.
  • check_circle Rumor Buster Feed: Official alerts and verified information published directly by the Police.
  • check_circle Multi-Language Support: Available in English, Hindi, and Marathi.

devices App Requirements

Target Users: All pilgrims, tourists, and Nashik visitors.
Supported Devices: Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer) or standard mobile web browser.
Internet: Works online; offline support for saved route fare cards.
Permissions: Location (to show nearby Ghats) and Camera (optional, only needed when uploading photo evidence).

KumbhVeer

KumbhVeer (Volunteer App)

For On-Ground Volunteers

help_outline What is it used for?

This app is used by on-ground student volunteers, NCC cadets, and NSS team members to review citizen reports, verify if overcharging or rumors are true, assist lost pilgrims, and escalate serious issues to the Police.

featured_play_list Key Features

  • check_circle Live Incident Queue: Shows pending pilgrim complaints nearby with full details and locations.
  • check_circle Severity Tagging: Mark issues as Normal, Urgent, or Critical to prioritize emergency cases.
  • check_circle On-Site Photo Proof: Capture and attach ground photos to verify or resolve an issue.
  • check_circle One-Tap Police Escalation: Instantly sends unresolvable or dangerous incidents to the Police Command desk.

devices App Requirements

Target Users: Registered volunteers, student teams, NCC cadets, and coordinators.
Supported Devices: Android smartphone (Android 8.0 or newer).
Internet: Active mobile data connection (4G / 5G / Wi-Fi).
Permissions: Camera (for photo verification) and GPS Location.
Access: Assigned Volunteer Badge / ID.

KumbhSetu-LocalBazaar

LocalBazaar (Nashikkar / Merchant)

For Local Merchants & Stalls

help_outline What is it used for?

This app is used by local Nashik shopkeepers, food stall owners, pooja item vendors, and dharamshalas to register their business, list their products at government-capped prices, show verified licenses, and connect directly with pilgrims.

featured_play_list Key Features

  • check_circle Stall & Business Profile: Register shop location, sector/zone, operating hours, and contact information.
  • check_circle Price-Capped Item Catalog: List essential goods (food, water, pooja items) within authorized price limits.
  • check_circle Trust & License Badges: Display FSSAI food hygiene registration and municipal vendor permits.
  • check_circle Customer Contact Management: Receive direct phone or message inquiries from pilgrims.

devices App Requirements

Target Users: Nashik merchants, food stalls, pooja stores, and accommodation providers.
Supported Devices: Android smartphone (Android 7.0 or newer).
Internet: Active mobile data or Wi-Fi.
Permissions: Camera / Storage (for stall photos and license documents).
Required Details: Shop name, owner name, mobile number, stall zone, and price list.

KumbhSetu-Admins

Admin & Police Command

For Police & Administration

help_outline What is it used for?

This software is used by the Police Commissionerate, Traffic Police, District Administration, and Municipal Officers to monitor incidents in real-time, dispatch field units, publish verified facts to stop panic, and set official fare and price ceilings.

featured_play_list Key Features

  • check_circle Incident Radar & Hotspot Map: View real-time complaints with severity levels and exact sector locations.
  • check_circle Rumor Buster Fact-Checker: Quickly tag and debunk false rumors, broadcasting official notices to all Yatri phones.
  • check_circle Route & Fare Tariff Control: Configure official per-kilometer and fixed route rates for autos, taxis, and buses.
  • check_circle Bazaar Price Ceiling Registry: Maintain maximum authorized retail prices for food, water, and pooja materials.

devices App Requirements

Target Users: Police Control Room, District Administration, and Municipal Officers.
Supported Devices: Windows Desktop / Laptop (Windows 10 / 11) or Android Tablet / Phone.
Internet: Stable broadband or high-speed mobile network.
Access: Official Admin / Police credentials.
