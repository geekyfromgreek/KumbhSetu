## 📸 Key Screen Walkthroughs

### 1. Yatri Portal (Pilgrim Interface)
* **Crowd Flow & Rate Ticker**: Real-time crowd meter for Ramkund and Trimbakeshwar ghats with live gazette tariffs.
* **Local Marketplace & Fair Booking**: Instant surge vs. gazette comparison across Auto Rickshaws, Hotels, Guides, and Food.
* **Food Finder**: Discover Free Annachhatra, Satvik, and Jain thalis with verified FSSAI hygiene grades.
* **Emergency SOS**: Instant access to 2,165 verified Nashik emergency coordinates.

### 2. Nashikkar Citizen & Vendor Portal
* **Trust Score Dashboard**: Real-time compliance score (98.4%) and active listing management.
* **Bookings Queue**: Real-time reservation requests with one-tap accept/reject and direct calling.
* **Price Flag Auditing**: Self-resolution portal for customer rate flags before police escalation.

### 3. Police Commissionerate & Administration
* **Live Escalation Feed**: High-priority surge gouging and rogue operator alerts.
* **Patrol Quick Dispatch**: One-click dispatch with route navigation.
* **Evidence Vault & Enforcement**: Comprehensive dossier with on-the-spot compound fine challans and permit suspensions.
